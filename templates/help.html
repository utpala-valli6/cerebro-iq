Help & Support

Learn how to use the CerebroIQ system

How to Analyze a CT Image

  1. Open Analyze Scan.
  2. Select a supported CT image.
  3. Click Analyze Scan.
  4. Wait for the AI model to process the image.
  5. View the screening result and confidence.

Supported Formats

CerebroIQ currently accepts JPG, JPEG and PNG image files.

Patient History

After an image is analyzed, the screening result is automatically stored in the local CerebroIQ history.

Important Notice

CerebroIQ is an educational and research AI screening project. Its output is not a medical diagnosis and should not replace evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional.
